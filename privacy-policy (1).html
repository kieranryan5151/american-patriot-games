Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Greenbush Games handles information when you use our websites and browser-based sports simulation games, including Wrestling Program Dynasty, Gaelic Games Manager, American Patriot Games, and GreenbushGames.com.

Information we collect

Greenbush Games does not require a user account to play our browser games. We do not intentionally ask players to provide personal information in order to use the games.

Our websites and service providers may automatically receive limited technical information associated with a visit, such as IP address, browser and device type, approximate geographic region, pages or resources requested, referral source, and request timing. This information is generally used for security, performance, troubleshooting, and traffic measurement.

Game saves and local storage

Some Greenbush Games titles save dynasty or game data locally in your browser using technologies such as browser storage or IndexedDB. Local save data is intended to remain on your device unless a feature explicitly tells you otherwise. Clearing browser data, changing browsers, or changing devices may remove locally stored saves.

Analytics and infrastructure

We use third-party infrastructure and analytics services, including Cloudflare, to deliver, secure, cache, and measure traffic to our websites. These providers may process technical information necessary to provide their services according to their own privacy policies.

Advertising and cookies

Greenbush Games may display advertising now or in the future. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads where permitted by law.

Where required, visitors may be presented with consent or privacy choices before certain advertising or analytics technologies are used. You may also be able to control cookies through your browser settings.

Third-party links

Our sites may link to third-party websites or services, including Reddit, X, GitHub, and other Greenbush Games websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party services.

Children's privacy

Our sites are general-audience sports simulation websites and are not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the issue.

Data retention

Greenbush Games does not maintain player accounts or a centralized database of local dynasty saves. Technical information handled by our hosting, security, analytics, or advertising providers may be retained according to those providers' policies and operational requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Greenbush Games adds features, analytics, advertising, or other services. The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to kieran@greenbushgames.com.
