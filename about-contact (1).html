About Greenbush Games

Independent browser-based sports simulation games

Greenbush Games creates free, browser-based sports management and simulation games built around deep fictional sports worlds, long-term careers, evolving records, rankings, recruiting, tournaments, and history.

The goal is to make the kind of detailed sports simulations that let players manage a program while also watching the wider game world develop around them over many seasons.

Our games

  • Wrestling Program Dynasty — manage a college wrestling program and RTC across NCAA folkstyle and international freestyle competition.
  • Gaelic Games Manager — a management simulation centered on Gaelic football and hurling.
  • American Patriot Games — a multi-sport simulation featuring athletes and teams representing American states and territories.
Greenbush Games is an independent project. References to real sports, schools, organizations, countries, or competition formats are used for simulation and informational context and do not imply endorsement or affiliation unless explicitly stated.
